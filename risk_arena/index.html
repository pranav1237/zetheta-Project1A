Scoring is a project-level heuristic (protection from structure, yield opportunity cost, and stressed-loss coverage), not a rating-agency methodology. It is designed to make the trade-off tangible: more credit enhancement and reserve improve resilience under stress but reduce the coupon a junior investor earns in the base case. See docs/securitisation/crisis_case_studies.md for the historical scenarios behind each preset, and src/securitisation/stress_testing.py for the equivalent calculation run against the real loan-level pool.
